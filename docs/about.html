About This Project

The Philosophy

This project applies several quantitative strategies to the US Stock Market to remove emotion from investing:

1. Momentum Engine

Focuses on MegaCap, S&P 500, and MidCap 400 cohorts.

  • Ranking: Stocks are ranked by 12-month volatility-adjusted returns.
  • Selection: Top 5 tickers in each cohort.

2. Munger Engine (Mean Reversion)

Focuses on high-quality Top 50 Market Cap stocks trading at a discount.

  • Signal: Price dips below the 200-day Moving Average (last 10 days) but recovers above the 10-day MA.
  • Selection: Opportunistic (all valid signals).

3. Munger400L and Munger400R (Report-Only)

Applies the same close-based mean-reversion signal to two independent S&P 400 screens.

  • Munger400L: The largest 15% of current constituents by MDY portfolio weight.
  • Munger400R: Stocks that ranked in the top 15% by 12-month return on a report date during the trailing year.
  • Signal: A close below the 200-day average during the last 10 sessions followed by a latest close above the 10-day average. The fixed 200-session window requires at least 90% observation coverage.

The Technology

Built with Python, using Polygon.io for data, SQLite for caching, and GitHub Actions for automation.
